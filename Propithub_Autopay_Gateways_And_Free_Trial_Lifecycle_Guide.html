Free Trial Lifecycle, Expiry & 15-Day Dropout Purge

Automated conversion to Starter ($29/mo) vs. 15-day countdown permanent deletion

Automated Daily Cron Engine

When an owner registers, they receive an initial free trial governed by SuperAdmin (0, 7, 14, or 30 days). The system handles two divergent paths upon expiration:

🟢

Path A: Autopay is ON

If the owner authorized a card or bank mandate during trial:

  • Zero Interruption: Account seamlessly promotes to planStatus: 'active'.
  • Auto-Debit: Automatically charges Base Plan (Starter @ $29/mo) via the saved live gateway mandate.
  • Platform Invoice: Creates official paid receipt record in platformInvoices.
  • Zero Data Loss: Portfolios, units, and tenant links remain permanently online.
🔴

Path B: Autopay is OFF (Trial Dropout)

If no mandate was registered, a strict 15-day purge countdown begins:

  • Immediate Lock: Status transitions to planStatus: 'trial_expired'.
  • Day 0 Email: Notifies owner that data will be purged in 15 days.
  • Day 14 Final Alert: Dispatches high-urgency SMS + Email (24h before deletion).
  • Day 15 Cascade Purge: Deep wipe deletes properties, units, files, and user record.

📅 Path B: 15-Day Dropout Purge Timeline

Day 0: Trial Expires & Purge Scheduled

T-15 Days

Cloud Function checkSubscriptionExpiry detects trial period has ended without Autopay. Marks planStatus = 'trial_expired' and sets purgeScheduledAt = now + 15 days. Dispatches Day 0 email alert.

Days 1 – 13: Read-Only Dashboard Lock

Grace Period

Owner sees persistent banner with remaining countdown days. Adding units or initiating leases is blocked by usePlanLimits until a subscription is activated.

Day 14: Final 24-Hour Emergency Warning

T-24 Hours

Automated Cloud Cron dispatches high-urgency Twilio SMS + SendGrid email warning that account, uploaded documents, and leases will be permanently erased at midnight.

Day 15 (Midnight): Cascade Account & Data Purge

PERMANENT DELETION

Cloud Function purgeExpiredTrialAccounts runs at midnight. Executes an atomic batch deletion:

// 1. Delete associated properties, units, tickets await db.collection('properties').where('ownerId', '==', ownerId).delete(); // 2. Delete tenant agreements and platform invoices await db.collection('agreements').where('ownerId', '==', ownerId).delete(); // 3. Delete owner record & Firebase Auth user await db.collection('owners').doc(ownerId).delete(); await admin.auth().deleteUser(ownerId);
⚠️ Protection Guard: Paying subscribers are never purged. If a paid plan expires, it transitions to past_due (protected indefinitely).
